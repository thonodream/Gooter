##Gooter - AI Disclosure and Policy

There are instances in the game thus far where AI (Artificial Intelligence) has been used either to improve existing human code or write small pieces of new code here and there. Some contributors might also use LLMs as a tool to assist with their work.

In terms of AI policy, verifiably all-human contributions are encouraged and prioritized, though use of LLMs aren't banned outright as long as you know what you are doing and you don't use chatbots to communicate with others or as a replacement for your own judgement and ideas.

While AI disclosures in your code contributions would be handy and appreciated by a lot of people, please do not advertise which model you are using.

Regardless of whether someone else chooses to use LLMs or not, please treat each other with kindness, respect, and abide by the Code of Conduct. No cause is ever great enough to justify hate/harassment campaigns against anyone who could very well be your peer. Also, please do not argue about AI usage in Issues where it doesn't belong.

AI will and shall never be used in this project for Music, Sounds, Art, Character Designs, Text, Ideas, or core gameplay and identity, neither in-house or by contributors.
